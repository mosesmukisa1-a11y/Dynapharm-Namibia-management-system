- Block contradictory approval, rejection, dispatch, cancellation and receipt actions.
- Enforce valid state transitions server-side.
- Preserve source and destination warehouse/branch scope.
- Preserve batch, expiry and document traceability.
- Prevent over-dispatch and over-receipt.
- Track partial dispatch, partial receipt and returned quantities correctly.
- Ensure retries do not duplicate movements.
- Link every movement to the originating request, transfer, dispatch or receipt document.

G. STOCK VALUATION

- Branch Sales must use the correct active DP price.
- Never infer missing DP or CP silently.
- Show missing valuation as an explicit data-quality exception.
- Historical transactions must retain transaction-time values.
- Add tests for sale, return, receipt, transfer, write-off and adjustment movements.
- Reconcile quantity multiplied by transaction-time unit value to movement totals.
- Confirm Finance, MIS, GM and Director reports use the intended valuation basis.

H. BATCH AND EXPIRY

- Preserve batch number and expiry date through receipt, transfer, dispatch, branch receipt, sale and return where required.
- Prevent selling unavailable or expired batches when policy forbids it.
- Define FEFO/FIFO behavior according to existing business rules.
- Report stock without batch history as a data-quality exception.
- Do not alter physical balances while repairing display-only batch-history gaps.
