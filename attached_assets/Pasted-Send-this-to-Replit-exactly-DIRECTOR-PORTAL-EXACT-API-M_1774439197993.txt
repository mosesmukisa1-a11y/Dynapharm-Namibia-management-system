# 6. EXACT MATH LOGIC FOR DIRECTOR PORTAL

Use these formulas exactly.

---

## A. Total Sales Revenue

```text
total_sales_revenue =
walkin_sales_revenue
+ member_sales_revenue
+ non_member_sales_revenue
+ online_fulfilled_sales_revenue
+ prescription_dispense_sales_revenue
+ consultation_fee_revenue
```

Only use:

* completed
* posted
* finalized transactions

Do not include drafts, pending, or unfulfilled orders.

---

## B. Monthly Revenue

```text
monthly_revenue =
all completed revenue within selected month/date range
```

---

## C. CIF Cost of Sales

```text
cif_cost_of_sales =
sum(quantity_sold_or_dispensed × unit_cif)
```

This should come from actual sold/dispensed items only.

Do not use current inventory stock for cost of sales.

---

## D. Gross Profit

```text
gross_profit = total_revenue_dp - cif_cost_of_sales
```

Where:

```text
total_revenue_dp = revenue based on DP-side internal sales logic
```

If consultation fees are included in top-line revenue, be explicit whether they sit inside or outside gross profit logic.

### Recommended:

* show consultation fees separately
* gross profit from stock-linked sales only
* optionally show “total operating revenue” separately

---

## E. Total Company Stock Quantity

```text
total_company_stock_qty =
total_warehouse_stock_qty
+ total_branch_stock_qty
+ total_field_stock_qty
+ total_in_transit_qty
```

If you do not track field separately, exclude it consistently.

---

## F. Total Company Stock DP Value

```text
total_company_stock_value_dp =
sum(closing_qty × unit_dp)
for all stock lines across company
```

Must use:

* exact branch balances
* exact warehouse balances
* exact batch lines
* DP only

---

## G. Total Warehouse Stock Quantity

```text
total_warehouse_stock_qty =
sum(closing_qty for all warehouse stock lines)
```

---

## H. Total Branch Stock Quantity

```text
total_branch_stock_qty =
sum(closing_qty for all branch stock lines)
```

---

## I. Total In-Transit Stock Quantity

```text
total_in_transit_qty =
sum(dispatched_qty not yet confirmed received)
```

---

## J. Total Sales Units

```text
total_sales_units =
sum(all completed sold units + dispensed units)
```

Use:

* walk-in sales units
* member sales units
* non-member sales units
* online fulfilled sales units
* prescription dispensed units

---

## K. Total Prescription Dispensed Units

```text
total_prescription_dispensed_units =
sum(actual dispensed qty only)
```

Do not use prescribed quantity.

---

## L. Total Write-Off Quantity

```text
total_writeoff_qty =
sum(all posted write-off quantities)
```

---

## M. Total Write-Off DP Value

```text
total_writeoff_value_dp =
sum(writeoff_qty × unit_dp)
```

---

## N. Total Low Stock Items

```text
total_low_stock_items =
count(stock lines where closing_qty <= low_stock_threshold)
```

Threshold must come from:

* product reorder level
  or
* system default threshold if not configured

---

## O. Total Expiring Items

```text
total_expiring_items =
count(stock lines where expiry_date <= today + threshold_days and closing_qty > 0)
```

Recommended threshold:

* 90 days
  or configured system alert threshold

---

## P. Revenue Contribution % by Branch

```text
branch_revenue_contribution_percent =
(branch_sales_revenue / total_company_sales_revenue) × 100
```

### Example

If total company revenue = N$100,000
and CBD revenue = N$62,000

Then:

```text
CBD contribution = 62%
```

---

## Q. Net Contribution After Expenses

This is very important for Director decisions.

```text
net_contribution_after_expenses =
gross_profit
- rent_expense
- monthly_overheads
- bonus_payout
```

This helps identify:

* burden DPCs
* asset DPCs
* branches to boost
* branches to eliminate

---

## R. Average Order Value

```text
avg_order_value =
total_sales_revenue / total_orders
```

Only use completed orders/transactions.

---

## S. Fast Moving Products

Rank by:

```text
sum(units_sold) desc
```

Optional secondary sort:

```text
sales_revenue desc
```

---

## T. Fast Moving Branches

Rank by:

* sales units
* sales revenue
* gross profit

Depending on chart selected.
