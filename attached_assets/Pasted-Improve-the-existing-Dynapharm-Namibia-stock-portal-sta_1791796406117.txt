Improve the existing Dynapharm Namibia stock portal, starting with the
Country Stock → Warehouse Distribution page.

First inspect the current code, APIs, database schema, permissions, and
transfer workflow. Preserve existing records and business rules. Identify
confirmed defects separately from suspected data issues.

LAYOUT
1. Use a collapsible left sidebar grouped into:
   - Overview
   - Inventory: Country Stock, Warehouses, Branch Stock, Batches
   - Transfers: Distribution, Warehouse Dispatch, Branch Transfers, Returns
   - Approvals: Refunds, Edit Requests, Counts and Write-offs
   - Reports: Stock, Cost, Valuation
   - Administration: relevant staff and configuration tools
   Preserve access to every existing feature.

2. Use a page header:
   Title: “Distribute stock”
   Subtitle: “Country Stock → Warehouse”
   Show the signed-in user, role, and relevant location scope.
   Keep unrelated branch filters off this form.

3. Show a simple workflow indicator:
   Create → Awaiting warehouse receipt → Received
   Explain the subsequent warehouse-to-branch process separately.
   Verify actual approval order from existing business rules before changing it.

4. Build a two-column desktop form:
   Left, approximately 65%:
   - Destination warehouse
   - Product search by name, SKU, or barcode
   - Searchable, paginated product table
   - Columns: Product, SKU, Available, Unit, Quantity, Add
   - Show all catalog products through search/pagination
   - Disable unavailable products with an explanation

   Right, approximately 35%, sticky:
   - Selected products
   - Editable quantities and Remove actions
   - Number of product lines and total units
   - Transfer note
   - Clear primary action: “Review transfer”
   - Final review displays source, destination, quantities, and note

5. Put transfer history below the form:
   Search plus destination, status, and date filters.
   Columns: Reference, Date, Destination, Product count, Total units,
   Status, Created by, Actions.
   Open a detail drawer for product lines, notes, and status history.
   Provide pagination and a clear empty state.

VISUAL STYLE
Use a light neutral background, white panels, dark readable text, and
Dynapharm green for primary actions. Use consistent icons, spacing,
sentence case, and readable product names. Use text labels alongside
status colors. Provide visible keyboard focus and labelled form fields.
On mobile, stack the form and keep the transfer summary easy to reach.

DEBUGGING AND DATA INTEGRITY
- Investigate the Received transfer containing zero units and no products.
- Investigate apparent duplicate product names using IDs and SKUs;
  never automatically merge or delete products.
- Verify whether the 100-product limit prevents selecting the remaining six.
- Standardize status display without changing historical meaning.
- Validate destination, product IDs, and positive whole-number quantities
  on both client and server.
- Enforce sufficient available stock on the server.
- Make stock updates atomic and protect against duplicate submissions
  and concurrent transfers.
- Verify when stock is reserved, deducted, received, or released after
  rejection; prevent double deduction or stock loss.
- Enforce role and location permissions on the server.
- Record who performed each action and when.
- Show loading, success, empty, and recoverable error states.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKS
Confirm every product is searchable; invalid or empty transfers cannot
submit; excessive quantities are rejected; double-clicking creates only
one transfer; concurrent transfers cannot overdraw stock; rejection
handles reservations correctly; and desktop/mobile layouts are usable.

Return the confirmed bugs, changes made, verification results, and any
remaining questions about business rules.
