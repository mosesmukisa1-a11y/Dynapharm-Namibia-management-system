Redesign the existing Dynapharm Namibia public landing page to feel
organized, welcoming, and professional.

Inspect the existing implementation first. Preserve working shop,
health check-up, outlet, distributor, and staff links.

BRAND AND VISUAL DIRECTION
Use Dynapharm Namibia’s RED AND WHITE theme.
Reuse the existing official logo and brand red where available.
If no brand colour is defined, use #C8102E as a provisional red.

- White should dominate the page.
- Use red for primary buttons, active navigation, and key accents.
- Use dark charcoal for readable body text.
- Use pale grey and very light red backgrounds to separate sections.
- Avoid large amounts of solid red or red body text.
- Use consistent typography, generous spacing, subtle borders, and
  restrained shadows.
- Use authentic product and branch photographs where available.
- Do not invent certifications, testimonials, or product benefits.

PAGE STRUCTURE

1. CLEAN HEADER
White header with the Dynapharm Namibia logo on the left.
Navigation:
Home | Products | Our Outlets | About | Health Check-Up

Use a prominent red “Shop Products” button.
Put Staff Login and Distributor Login in a small secondary menu.
Keep the header compact and use a clear mobile menu.

2. FOCUSED HERO
Use a two-column layout:
Left: short headline, supporting text, and two actions.
Right: a high-quality authentic product or branch photograph.

Suggested headline:
“Natural wellness, closer to home.”

Suggested supporting text:
“Explore Dynapharm products and find your nearest outlet in Namibia.”

Primary red button: “Shop Products”
Secondary outlined button: “Find an Outlet”

Keep the existing “Health, Wealth & Freedom” brand slogan as a small
supporting line. Avoid repeating similar headlines.
On mobile, stack the content with the headline and actions first.
Avoid an automatic image carousel.

3. SIMPLE QUICK LINKS
Below the hero, show three compact cards:
- Explore Products
- Find an Outlet
- Health Check-Up

Use a consistent icon, one short explanation, and one clear link per card.
For Health Check-Up, accurately explain the service and link to its
existing booking or information page.

4. PRODUCT CATEGORIES
Show a clean grid of the actual product categories.
Each card should contain an appropriate image or icon, category name,
and a “Browse” link.

Make category cards open the relevant filtered product listing.
Verify product classifications, especially Compact Powder.
Keep product categories driven by the existing catalog.

5. FEATURED PRODUCTS
Show only 4–6 products on the homepage.
Use consistent image proportions and readable product names.
Display category and pack size; show price only when reliable live
pricing exists.

Use “View product” actions and a “View all products” link.
Only label products “Popular” or “Best sellers” if supported by data.
Remove generic health-benefit descriptions unless verified and approved.
Keep full catalog search and filters on the Products page.

6. SHORT ABOUT SECTION
Use one authentic image and a concise paragraph about Dynapharm Namibia.
Include a “Learn about us” link.
Show only verified facts, such as outlet coverage or years established.
Remove unsupported claims about scientific backing, certifications,
or quality testing until evidence is supplied.

7. OUTLET FINDER PREVIEW
Replace the long homepage address list with a compact outlet finder:
- Search by town or outlet name
- Optional region filter
- Cards showing outlet name, address, and phone
- “Call” and “Directions” actions

Show a small initial selection with “View all outlets”.
Keep the complete directory on the Our Outlets page.
Use verified map destinations; do not invent coordinates.
Review outlet region assignments for accuracy.

8. FINAL CALL TO ACTION
Use a restrained red section with white text:
“Find your next Dynapharm product.”
Include “Browse Products” and “Find an Outlet” buttons.
Keep wording concise and avoid promising health outcomes.

9. ORGANIZED FOOTER
Use clear columns:
- Brand and short description
- Customer links
- Distributor and staff access
- Head-office contact details

Use recognizable social icons with accessible names and working links.
Remove placeholder social links.
Clarify or remove “Cloud Health Management System” if it is unrelated
to the public website.

and a logical heading structure.
