 stock management system that tracks products from import and warehouse receipt through distribution to branches, sales, returns, stock adjustments, and reporting.

The system should cover the following processes:

Imports and receiving stock

Record purchase orders, suppliers, shipments, and imported products.

Capture quantities ordered, received, damaged, or missing.

Calculate product costs, including freight, customs duties, and other import expenses.

Record product barcodes, batch numbers, and expiry dates.

Warehouse stock management

Track stock held in each warehouse.

Record every stock receipt, transfer, return, and adjustment.

Separate available, reserved, damaged, and expired stock.

Support physical stock counts and explain differences between counted and recorded quantities.

Distribution to branches

Record stock dispatched from warehouses to branches.

Track stock in transit until the receiving branch confirms receipt.

Identify shortages, damage, or differences between dispatched and received quantities.

Support transfers between warehouses and between branches.

Branch sales and stock deductions

Record sales by product, quantity, price, branch, and date.

Automatically deduct sold quantities from the correct branch’s stock.

Use barcode scanning to identify products accurately.

Handle cancelled sales and refunds without duplicating stock movements.

Returns

Handle customer returns, branch returns to warehouses, and returns to suppliers.

Link returns to the original transaction where possible.

Record the reason, quantity, condition, and refund or credit.

Add saleable returns back to available stock and separate damaged or expired returns.

Stock adjustments and calculations

Record deductions for damage, expiry, theft, loss, and internal use.

Require reasons and an audit trail for manual adjustments.

Calculate stock for each product, location, and batch using:

Closing stock = Opening stock + Receipts + Transfers in + Saleable returns − Sales − Transfers out − Supplier returns − Write-offs ± Other adjustments

Prevent double counting when stock moves between locations.

Expiry management

Track expiry dates by batch.

Prioritise selling or dispatching stock that expires first.

Alert staff about products approaching expiry.

Record expired stock and its removal from available inventory.

Barcodes and product information

Maintain a product catalogue with unique product codes and barcodes.

Support different pack sizes and units, such as individual items, boxes, and cartons.

Convert quantities correctly when receiving cartons and selling individual items.

Link stock movements to the correct product and, where required, its batch.

Sales and stock reporting

Show current stock by product, warehouse, branch, and batch.

Report sales quantities, revenue, returns, and gross profit.

Show stock movement history and stock valuation.

Identify low stock, out-of-stock products, slow-moving products, and stock approaching expiry.

Allow reports to be filtered by date, product, supplier, and location.
