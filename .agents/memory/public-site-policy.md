---
name: Public website brand and evidence rules
description: Public red-and-white branding, catalogue consistency, and confirmation boundaries.
---
The public Dynapharm Namibia website uses red and white, with the existing official logo. Keep staff and stock-portal design work separate.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a white-led, welcoming public site rather than the stock portal's visual theme.

**How to apply:** Preserve shop, health questionnaire, distributor and staff entry points; use charcoal body text and restrained red accents.

Public claims, classifications, outlet totals and photographs require supporting evidence. Internal branch records alone do not establish active public outlets; unresolved entries should remain separately available for confirmation, not deleted or silently counted as verified.

**Why:** The public directory and internal location list disagreed, and Compact Powder's stored health-supplement classification could not be verified.

**How to apply:** Keep public catalogue/count eligibility consistent across landing and shop. Preserve IDs/SKUs and official names, do not merge apparent duplicates automatically, and do not invent certifications, benefits, best-seller claims or pack sizes.
